  Dataset Description;
 The dataset contains transactional sales records from 2023 to 2025, including information related to products, payment methods, order status, referral sources, quantities, and total sales values.

 Analysis Performed;
The following analyses were conducted:
- Product sales analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Order status analysis
- Referral source analysis
- Yearly sales trend analysis
- Basic statistical analysis
- Outlier identification

  Key Insights;
  Product Performance(Product Sales Chart.png)
    Chair recorded the highest sales performance in the dataset, followed closely by Printer with only a slight difference in      total sales. This indicates strong customer demand for both product categories.
  Payment Method Analysis(Payment Method Chart.png)
    Online payment was the most frequently used payment method, followed by Cash transactions. This suggests that customers       preferred digital payment options for completing purchases.
  Order Status Analysis(Order Status Chart.png)
   Cancelled orders appeared most frequently in the dataset, followed by Returned orders. This may indicate operational          inefficiencies,customer dissatisfaction, or order fulfillment challenges that could negatively impact business performance.
  Referral Source Analysis(Referral Source Chart.png)
    Instagram generated the highest customer referrals, followed by Email marketing. 
  Yearly Sales Trend(Yearly Sales Trend Chart)
    The dataset shows that 2022 recorded the highest sales performance, while 2025 had the lowest sales volume within the         dataset range. This may suggest a decline in sales performance over time or incomplete records for later periods.
